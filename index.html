For My Beautiful Girl

Read this slowly. Every word is meant for you.

Happy Birthdayyyy, my babyyyy. ❤️🥹

I honestly don’t even know where to start because every time I try to put how I feel about you into words, I realize there’s just too much to say.

But today is your day, so let me try.

Happy birthday, my heart. ❤️

Another year of you.
Another year of the beautiful, stubborn, dramatic, caring, overthinking, ridiculously adorable girl that somehow found her way into my heart and decided to stay there.

And honestly, I’m glad you did.

❤️ • ❤️ • ❤️

Sometimes I sit back and think about us and how strange it is that we got here.

Because there was a time when you were just someone I talked to. Then you became someone I looked forward to talking to. Then somehow, without me even realizing it, you became someone I started looking for in everything.

A person I wanted to tell things to. A person I wanted to hear from. A person whose mood could affect mine. A person whose “good morning” could genuinely make my morning better.

And now look at us.

Look at you.

My girl. ❤️

I don’t think you realize how many little things about you I’ve fallen in love with.

The way you talk. The way you laugh. The way you get excited about something and suddenly I’m listening to the whole story even if I had no idea what you were talking about five minutes ago.

The way you can be so sweet one minute and then annoy me the next. 😭

The way you overthink. The way you care so deeply. The way you sometimes need reassurance even when you already know the answer. The way you miss me and somehow make me miss you even more.

I love the little things.

I love our random conversations. Our calls. Our stupid jokes. Our arguments that somehow end with us missing each other. The moments where neither of us really has anything important to say but we still don’t want to hang up.

I love how comfortable you’ve become with me.

And I hope you know how comfortable I’ve become with you too.

You became a place I could just be myself.

No pretending. No trying too hard. Just me and you.

And maybe that’s one of the things I love most about us.

We can be silly. We can be serious. We can talk for hours. We can annoy each other. We can miss each other. We can be quiet.

And somehow, it’s still us.

💗 • 💗 • 💗

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel special.

And I don’t know if I’ll ever be able to properly explain what that means to me.

But since it’s your birthday, I’m going to try.

I want you to know that I’m proud of you.

Not just for the things you’ve achieved, but for the person you’re becoming.

I’ve seen pieces of you that not everyone gets to see. I’ve seen the soft side. The emotional side. The stubborn side. The vulnerable side. The side that overthinks everything. The side that pretends she’s okay sometimes.

And I want you to know that you never have to pretend with me.

If you’re happy, I want to celebrate with you.

If you’re sad, I want to sit with you.

If you’re confused, I’ll listen.

If you’re scared, I’ll remind you that you don’t have to figure everything out alone.

And if you’re being dramatic…

Well.

I’ll probably still call you “ma” and let Your Royal Highness have her moment. 😭❤️

But seriously, I hope this new year of your life is gentle with you.

I hope you get everything you’ve been praying for. I hope you grow into the woman you’re meant to become. I hope you experience happiness that doesn’t have an expiration date. I hope you laugh until your stomach hurts. I hope you travel. I hope you make beautiful memories. I hope you discover new things about yourself. And I hope you always remember that you are deeply loved.

And selfishly…

I hope I’m there for a lot of those memories.

I want more random calls. More late-night conversations. More stupid arguments. More laughing until we can’t breathe. More “I miss you.” More “come here.” More moments where we look at each other and don’t even need to say anything.

And yes…

I still think about that first kiss. 😭❤️

Because tell me why we’ve talked about it so many times that we’ve practically written the entire scene already.

We’ve imagined the moment. We’ve talked about how nervous we’d probably be. We’ve joked about who would make the first move.

And honestly?

I don’t know what’s going to happen when that moment finally comes.

Maybe we’ll both be nervous. Maybe we’ll laugh. Maybe we’ll stare at each other for way too long. Maybe one of us will suddenly forget every single thing we’ve ever said about it. 😭

But I do know one thing.

When it finally happens, I want it to be one of those moments neither of us forgets.

Not because it has to be perfect.

But because it’s ours.

After all the conversations. After all the teasing. After all the “what ifs.” After all the times we’ve imagined it.

Just us.

And knowing us, we’ll probably laugh immediately afterwards because we’ll both be too shy to act normal. 😭❤️

But honestly, baby…

I’m looking forward to that moment.

❤️ • ❤️ • ❤️

I’m looking forward to so many moments with you.

The first time we get to spend an entire day together. The first proper date. The first time we take pictures together and actually look like an annoying couple. The first time we fall asleep beside each other. The first time we look at each other and realize, “Damn… we really made it here.”

I want all of those memories with you.

Because you’re not just someone I want to love in the big moments.

I want you in the ordinary ones too.

The boring Tuesdays. The random afternoons. The sleepy calls. The “what are you eating?” conversations. The days where nothing happens.

I want the simple things.

Because with you, I think even the simple things would mean something.

And if I could give you one thing for your birthday, it wouldn’t be something I could wrap.

I’d give you the ability to see yourself through my eyes for just one day.

Maybe then you’d understand why I look at you the way I do.

Maybe you’d understand why your smile gets me. Why your voice calms me. Why your presence matters to me. Why I miss you even when we literally just finished talking.

Maybe you’d finally understand why I call you my girl.

Because you are.

You’re my girl. My favorite person to annoy. My favorite person to talk to. My favorite person to miss. My favorite person to hear from. And somehow, one of my favorite parts of my life.

💖 • 💖 • 💖

So today, I don’t just want to say happy birthday.

I want to say thank you.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for trusting me. Thank you for letting me know the real you. Thank you for every conversation. Thank you for every laugh. Thank you for every late-night call. Thank you for every little memory we’ve already made.

And thank you for all the memories we haven’t made yet.

Because I know we’re not done.

There are still so many firsts waiting for us.

So many places. So many conversations. So many laughs. So many hugs. So many pictures. So many “I miss yous.” And yes…

That first kiss we’ve talked about approximately 847 times. 😭❤️

I hope this birthday reminds you of how special you are.

Not just to me, but to everyone lucky enough to have you in their life.

But if I’m being completely honest…

I hope you always know that you have a very special place in mine.

I love you, baby.

More than I probably know how to explain properly.

And I hope as you start this new chapter, you remember one thing:

No matter how much you grow, how much changes, or how many birthdays come after this one…

I’ll always be grateful that somewhere along the way, I found you.

💋❤️
